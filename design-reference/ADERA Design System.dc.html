06 · Voice & principles

Plain language always
"Why this fits you," never "RAG." "Held until you approve the proof," never "escrow milestone capture."
Trust through transparency
Source link-outs, confidence dots, cited eligibility, what-we-checked expanders, one-tap error report.
Timezone honesty
Every deadline shows the user's local time with EAT alongside. Countdowns, not just dates.
