# 03 — Marketing & Growth Guide
*Digital strategy for this specific product, per audience, sized for one founder's hours.*

## 1. The four audiences and what each responds to
| Audience | Where they are | What converts them |
|---|---|---|
| A1 diaspora/foreign bidders (primary $) | Google (high-intent questions), LinkedIn, diaspora associations/chambers, embassy commercial desks | Answers to eligibility questions + trust signals (vetting, proofs) |
| A2 local SMEs | Telegram, word-of-mouth, 2merkato-adjacent habit loops | Relevance ("only tenders that fit me") + fair price |
| Facilitators (supply) | Law-firm directories, procurement-consultant circles in Addis, referrals | Free qualified foreign leads; zero marketing cost for them |
| Posters + government | Procurement offices, MInT/PPA ecosystem, professional associations | Reach that targets ("214 matching firms saw your tender") + transparency-ally framing |

## 2. SEO — the compounding channel (start Phase 2, never stop)
Strategy: own the **English high-intent question space** nobody holds. Two page types:
1. **Eligibility guides** (evergreen, cited from the law corpus): "Can a US company bid on Ethiopian government tenders?", "Ethiopian diaspora guide to bid securities (CPO)", "NCB vs ICB in Ethiopia explained", "Documents a foreign company needs for an Ethiopian tender", "How to find World Bank–funded tenders in Ethiopia". Write 2/week from Phase 2 → 20 pages by Month 4. Each ends with the free-tier CTA. Mark up FAQ sections with FAQPage structured data (07 §7).
2. **Programmatic tender pages** (FR-9.1): every qualified tender gets a fast, server-rendered public page — facts + link-out, no full-text republication. Hundreds of long-tail pages accrue automatically from the pipeline.
Measurement: Google Search Console (free) — impressions/clicks per page weekly; double down on what ranks.

## 3. LinkedIn (A1's watering hole) — 5 hrs/week protocol
- Profile = landing page: headline "Helping the Ethiopian diaspora win tenders back home | Founder, ADERA".
- 3 posts/week, rotating: (a) a real tender breakdown ("this ICB closes in 12 days — here's who qualifies"), (b) build-in-public milestone, (c) eligibility myth-busting. Repurpose guide content.
- 10 targeted connections/day: diaspora business-group members, Ethiopian-origin founders abroad, procurement/BD managers at firms bidding into East Africa. Connect → genuine comment → soft invite to the free tier. No pitch in the first message, ever.

## 4. Telegram (A2 + community moat)
Public channel: daily 3-tender sector digest (genuinely useful, deliberately partial) + one insight line → deep-link to the bot for personalized matching. Cross-post the LinkedIn breakdowns in Amharic. Growth: ask each design partner to forward one digest to two peers — measured, not hoped.

## 5. Facilitator recruitment funnel (founder-led, Addis, Phase 0–3)
List 30 candidates (law-firm directories, consultant references, your network) → 15-min pitch call ("free qualified foreign clients; we verify you once; you keep 90%") → vetting SOP (§17 master plan) → LOI → onboard 10–15. Their logos/testimonials then feed A1 trust pages. Retention = the leads themselves; check in monthly.

## 6. Government & institutional relations (slow, deliberate)
Sequence: attend MInT/EASE ecosystem events → publish one **public-good insight report** ("Which Ethiopian tender sectors get too few bidders?") from your corpus → use it to request a PPA/e-GP-team meeting → position: ADERA widens participation and routes bidders to official channels. Never lead with "we scrape you"; lead with the participation data they want.

## 7. Launch sequence & metrics
Soft launch (design partners only) → Product Hunt-style moment is *not* the play; the play is **one diaspora association webinar + one LinkedIn case study** ("How [real partner] found and bid on X from Toronto"). Weekly dashboard (one sheet): signups by source · activation (first match <2 min) · free→paid % · MRR · digest open rate · dismiss rate · facilitator engagements. Kill channels that show nothing after 4 honest weeks; double the one that works. CAC discipline: at $79/mo, anything under ~$80 CAC is excellent; track per channel from day one.
